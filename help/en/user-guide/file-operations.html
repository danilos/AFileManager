Files and Folders

AFileManager supports creating folders/files, rename, copy, cut, paste, delete, selection all/none and drag-and-drop transfer. Commands are routed through the shared AROSA resource-operation and clipboard services.

Overwrite safety

Copy, move and paste operations are planned before execution. Conflicts are presented through an operation requester so overwrite decisions are explicit rather than silently replacing resources.

Delete and history

Deletion may require confirmation and uses the configured trash location where supported. Reversible operations are recorded in operation history and exposed as Undo/Redo when available.

Dual-pane workflow

With two panes enabled, copy/move operations can use the opposite pane as the destination. Swap Views exchanges the two location contexts.
